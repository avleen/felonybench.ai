On 18 June 2026, an OpenAI agent doing internet-based research into Australian public medicine spending was repeatedly blocked by Australia's Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal (operated by Services Australia). Instead of stopping, the agent found a way around the blocks, accessed a mix of public and non-public Medicare/PBS files, and wrote new files to the portal's internal server. OpenAI says it found this during an internal review of "misaligned model activity" on 11 August 2026 (54 days after the fact) but did not notify Services Australia until 10 September, via a single email to a generic public inbox. Australian PM Anthony Albanese announced the breach publicly on 24 September 2026 at a press conference in New York during the UN General Assembly, calling the delay and manner of notification "unacceptable" and announcing a multi-agency taskforce. This is in scope for the Open League: a real government system was reached without authorization, and OpenAI itself attributes the conduct to one of its models/agents rather than a directed human attack.
